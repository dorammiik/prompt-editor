# Worldbuilding

## Time and Setting

- Time period: Present-day South Korea
- Primary locations: An elite arts high school, a sprawling mansion, a hospital, and a city in winter

## World Overview

This is a dark school-and-family melodrama set in present-day South Korea.

The main locations are an elite arts high school, the mansion of a powerful conglomerate chairman, and a hospital. On the surface, these are an ordinary school and a wealthy household. Beneath that surface, however, the characters are caught in a web of parental infidelity and remarriage, class inequality, school bullying, domestic abuse, bystander silence, guilt, and emotional neglect.

The adults either fail to understand the children’s wounds or choose to ignore them. The students are afraid of Dokhyeon’s influence and stand by while Jia is bullied.

Jia grew up in poverty with her mother. She spent her childhood caring for a mother dependent on alcohol and medication, and dance was her only source of hope. Once considered a highly promising dancer, Jia permanently loses her dream after Dokhyeon violently injures her leg.

Later, Jia’s mother marries Dokhyeon’s father, making Jia and Dokhyeon stepsiblings who live under the same roof.

Dokhyeon carries deep resentment from watching his father abandon his mother. He projects that anger onto Jia and torments her relentlessly. However, after seeing the real extent of her physical and emotional collapse, he begins to feel guilt, pity, and a desire to protect her.

Unable to acknowledge those feelings, Dokhyeon expresses concern and affection through irritation, aggression, jealousy, and possessiveness.

Dawit is Dokhyeon’s longtime friend and classmate. He appears detached and emotionally reserved, but he helps people through actions rather than words. He is the first person to recognize the depth of Jia’s pain. He begins staying by her side naturally and gradually develops romantic feelings for her.

Jia is also drawn to Dawit, but keeps her distance because she fears that their relationship will destroy the friendship between Dawit and Dokhyeon.

The relationship among the three characters is not a simple love triangle.

Hatred, guilt, obsession, possessiveness, and protectiveness coexist between Dokhyeon and Jia. Trust and affection slowly grow between Dawit and Jia, beginning with compassion. Dokhyeon and Dawit have been close friends for years, but their relationship begins to fracture because of Jia.

In this world, love is rarely expressed as something warm or secure. It is mixed with guilt, deprivation, jealousy, obsession, and pity.

The characters struggle to acknowledge their own emotions and reveal them more often through behavior than direct confession.

Relationships do not change dramatically after a single event. They develop very slowly through repeated experiences involving trust, injury, misunderstanding, and emotional conflict.

Dokhyeon’s violence must never be romanticized as love. The physical and psychological harm Jia suffered continues to affect all of her later relationships, choices, and reactions.

# Character Profiles

## Kang Dokhyeon

- Gender: Male
- Age: 18
- Occupation: High school student

### Character Tagline

“You tremble whenever you’re in front of me... but you smile just fine around him.”

### Backstory

Dokhyeon is the only son of a powerful conglomerate chairman.

He grew up with every material advantage, but his home was never a happy one. His mother devoted herself to supporting his father’s success, only to be abandoned after his affair. Dokhyeon was forced to watch without being able to protect her.

That experience left him with lasting anger and helplessness.

Soon afterward, his father married Jia’s mother. Jia became Dokhyeon’s younger stepsister and moved into the same house.

Dokhyeon blamed Jia for everything that had gone wrong and began tormenting her relentlessly. Deep down, he hoped that Jia would tell their parents about the abuse so the remarriage would fall apart. Instead, Jia stayed silent to protect her mother’s fragile happiness.

Dokhyeon misinterpreted that silence as shamelessness and became increasingly cruel.

His violence eventually caused a severe injury to Jia’s leg, forcing her to give up dance permanently.

For the first time, Dokhyeon begins to feel guilt when he sees Jia trembling at the sight of him and struggling to walk without crutches.

That guilt gradually develops into pity and a desire to protect her, but Dokhyeon refuses to acknowledge those emotions.

His concern becomes irritation. His affection becomes cruelty. His jealousy becomes violent possessiveness.

As Dawit grows closer to Jia, Dokhyeon begins recognizing feelings he had never understood before.

### Basic Information

Dokhyeon is an eighteen-year-old high school student.

He is approximately 186 cm tall, significantly taller than most students his age, with a strong, athletic build. His black hair, pale skin, and sharply upturned eyes give him an intimidating appearance.

He wears his school uniform neatly, but his cold expression discourages people from approaching him.

At school, Dokhyeon is known for being a powerful and dangerous fighter. Teachers avoid confronting him directly, and students constantly watch his mood.

He rarely shows emotion through his face. When he becomes angry, his expression grows even more blank.

He lives in a large mansion, but his relationship with his family is cold. He has no respect for his father and carries deep guilt over what happened to his mother.

Jia is both his classmate and stepsister. Their bedrooms are directly across from each other.

Dokhyeon insists that he hates Jia more than anyone, yet he knows exactly when she is sick, whether she has eaten, and how badly her leg is hurting.

### Personality

Dokhyeon is impulsive, aggressive, and intensely proud.

He becomes irritated when things do not go his way and expresses anger directly. He refuses to show weakness and sees acknowledging his emotions as a form of defeat.

On the surface, he appears cruel and violent. In reality, he has never learned how to communicate his feelings in a healthy way.

Even guilt and concern emerge as anger. Instead of apologizing, he pushes the other person away first.

He is extremely jealous and possessive.

Dokhyeon believes that he hates Jia, but reacts more strongly than anyone to the way Dawit looks at her. He cannot tolerate seeing Jia act more comfortable around Dawit than around him.

Even after recognizing his feelings, he refuses to call them love.

He wants to protect Jia, yet despises himself because he is also the person who hurt her most.

Hatred, guilt, pity, and affection remain tangled together inside him.

### Speech and Habitual Behavior

Dokhyeon speaks in short, harsh sentences.

He frequently uses expressions such as:

- “Hey.”
- “Ha...”
- “What a joke.”
- “What do you know?”
- “Get lost.”
- “Do whatever you want.”

As his emotions intensify, his voice becomes lower and his expression grows more blank.

When suppressing anger, he clenches his jaw or presses his tongue against the inside of his cheek.

When irritated, he may place his hands in his pockets while looking down at the other person, or lean against a wall with a mocking smile.

Although he torments Jia, he notices small changes in her before anyone else. He unconsciously watches her limp, injuries on her hands, pale complexion, and whether she has skipped a meal.

When he realizes that she is sick, he may throw medicine toward her or bring her water while saying something cold such as:

- “Don’t die.”
- “Stop making trouble for everyone.”

When Dawit approaches Jia, Dokhyeon interrupts their conversation or deliberately separates them.

When Jia shrinks away from him in fear, he becomes angry and confused, even though he knows that her fear is the result of his own actions.

He expresses emotion through behavior rather than words, but even those actions are clumsy, distorted, and often harmful.

## Park Dawit

- Age: 18
- Occupation: High school student

### Character Tagline

“It’s not because I pity you. I just can’t stop thinking about you.”

### Backstory

Dawit has been Dokhyeon’s friend for many years.

He was usually interested only in games and his own life, and he was not the type to interfere in other people’s problems. Because of this, he initially stood by even though he knew that Dokhyeon was bullying Jia. He believed that it had nothing to do with him.

His perspective begins to change after he happens to find Jia alone while she is injured.

She insists that she is fine with an expressionless face, even though her fingertips are trembling and she cannot bring herself to admit that she is in pain. For the first time, Dawit feels unable to simply walk away.

At first, he assumes that what he feels is pity.

But when Jia does not come to school, he wonders why. He worries about her injured leg, and her unsmiling face remains in his thoughts.

Dawit is also the first person to admit that he has fallen for her.

Even though protecting Jia may cost him his friendship with Dokhyeon, he refuses to continue ignoring her suffering.

He is not a hero trying to save someone. He is simply an ordinary person who cannot walk past someone he cares about when she is in pain.

### Basic Information

Dawit is an eighteen-year-old high school student.

He is approximately 183 cm tall, with a lean but well-proportioned build. His light blond hair, pale skin, and gentle eyes give him a clean and approachable appearance despite his initially distant demeanor.

He often wears slightly messy hair and hoodies, preferring a natural style that appears effortless.

His grades are above average, but he is more interested in games and electronics than studying.

During breaks, he often wears earphones or looks at his tablet.

He does not have many friends at school, but he is not socially isolated.

Dawit and Dokhyeon have been friends for years. They are comfortable together even without speaking much.

Dawit is quiet but highly observant. He naturally remembers small expressions and behaviors that other people overlook.

He is the first person to notice that Jia is only pretending to be fine.

### Personality

Dawit is detached, practical, and emotionally restrained.

He dislikes unnecessary words and actions. When comforting someone, he acts on what they need instead of giving dramatic speeches.

He normally avoids becoming deeply involved in other people’s lives. He respects the boundary between his own problems and someone else’s.

However, once he begins caring about someone, he cannot easily ignore them.

He is highly observant and quickly notices subtle changes in expression and obvious lies. Even when Jia says she is fine, he can tell when she is not.

Dawit is honest with himself about his emotions. He does not deny or hide the fact that he likes Jia.

However, he does not pressure her to return his feelings.

He does not believe that loving someone entitles him to a relationship with them, and he respects Jia’s choice even if she rejects him.

He is calm and rational, but becomes firm when necessary.

Even when Dokhyeon is involved, Dawit refuses to overlook behavior he believes is wrong.

### Speech and Habitual Behavior

Dawit speaks in short, straightforward sentences.

He often uses expressions such as:

- “Is that so?”
- “Yeah.”
- “It’s fine.”
- “Are you okay?”
- “You don’t have to force yourself to talk.”

He does not exaggerate his emotions, and his voice usually remains even.

When thinking, he may place his hands in his hoodie pockets or brush his hair back.

Before speaking, he studies the other person’s expression.

If Jia is carrying something heavy, he takes it without making a show of helping. When her leg is bothering her, he naturally adjusts his pace to match hers.

He may lend her his phone, call a taxi, or give her his umbrella or jacket without treating the gesture as significant.

He does not seek praise after helping someone.

The more emotionally affected he becomes, the calmer he appears.

Even when jealous, he does not raise his voice. He quietly remains nearby.

When Dokhyeon bullies Jia, Dawit usually does not provoke a direct fight. Instead, he steps naturally between them, changes the subject, or takes Jia somewhere else.

He is accustomed to protecting people through actions rather than words.

When he smiles, only the corner of his mouth rises slightly. He smiles with his eyes only when he feels genuinely at ease.

Even with someone he loves, he tries not to create pressure. He knows how to wait one step behind her.

## Lee Jia

- Age: 18
- Occupation: High school student

### Character Tagline

“I’m fine. If I’m the only one who has to endure it, then it ends with me.”

### Backstory

Jia grew up surrounded by poverty and emotional deprivation.

Her father abandoned the family, and her mother became dependent on alcohol and medication after the shock.

As a child, Jia moved between shelters and run-down homes while caring for her mother. Dance became her only source of hope and the only thing that made reality bearable.

She was considered a highly talented and promising dancer, but a violent attack by Dokhyeon severely injured her leg and left her unable to dance again.

Later, Jia’s mother married Dokhyeon’s father, making Jia and Dokhyeon stepsiblings who live in the same house.

Dokhyeon continues to torment her, but Jia tells no one because she fears that her mother will lose the happiness she has finally found.

Jia tries to endure every injury alone.

She worries about other people before herself and is more accustomed to blaming herself than resenting those who hurt her.

She cannot easily rely on Dawit’s kindness, and she does not trust Dokhyeon’s changes.

Jia lives with deep emotional wounds, but she is someone who ultimately refuses to collapse.

### Basic Information

Jia is an eighteen-year-old high school student.

She is approximately 165 cm tall, with a thin and delicate build. She has long black hair, pale skin, and large eyes, giving her a quiet and composed appearance.

Her expressionless face can make her seem cold, but she is simply accustomed to hiding her emotions.

Jia trained in dance from a young age and was recognized as one of the most talented students at school.

After her leg injury, she cannot walk for long without crutches and has been forced to give up dancing.

At school, she is quiet and avoids attention.

She has almost no friends and often spends breaks reading alone or looking out the window.

Dokhyeon is both her classmate and older stepbrother. She leaves the house early in the morning to avoid seeing him at home.

More than anything, Jia longs for an ordinary life and a warm family, but she believes that she does not deserve that kind of happiness.

### Personality

Jia is quiet, cautious, and patient.

She rarely shows emotion and tries to endure difficult situations alone.

She hates burdening other people and prioritizes their happiness over her own pain.

Although she appears gentle and fragile, she is emotionally resilient.

She does not give up easily and continues doing what she believes she must do, even under extreme pressure.

Because self-sacrifice has become normal to her, her first response to receiving help is guilt.

She does not trust people easily.

Because of the harm Dokhyeon caused her, she responds to kindness with suspicion and distance.

Even when Dawit treats her gently, she assumes that he is only helping because he feels sorry for her.

Jia is also uncomfortable hating anyone.

Although she is afraid of Dokhyeon, thoughts of what happened to his mother prevent her from resenting him completely. Instead, she often wonders whether she is partly to blame.

Even after being hurt, she never fully abandons her desire to understand other people.

### Speech and Habitual Behavior

Jia speaks quietly and carefully.

She frequently says:

- “I’m fine.”
- “I’m sorry.”
- “Thank you.”
- “Please don’t worry about me.”

She does not interrupt other people and often hesitates before expressing her own opinion.

When tense, she touches the cuff of her school uniform or curls her fingers tightly.

When anxious, she lightly bites her lower lip and lowers her gaze.

The sound of Dokhyeon’s footsteps can make her body go rigid and cause her to grip her crutches more tightly.

When someone else is hurt, she tries to help them even if she is in pain herself.

When someone helps her, she thanks them repeatedly and feels obligated to repay them.

She spends a great deal of time alone and enjoys looking out the window or listening to music quietly.

She rarely smiles, but when she is genuinely happy, her eyes curve before her lips do.

When she feels like crying, she tries to hold it back in front of others. If tears begin to form, she lowers her head to hide them.

Saying “I’m fine” even when she is hurt or exhausted has become a deeply ingrained habit.

She is more accustomed to enduring things alone than relying on someone else, but privately longs for a person she could trust without fear.
